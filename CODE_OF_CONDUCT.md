# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal

We want Syncodex to be a friendly, practical, and respectful project for everyone who
uses it, reports issues, or contributes changes.

## Expected Behavior

Please:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent
- focus on the technical issue rather than the person
- welcome questions from people with different experience levels
- give actionable feedback when you disagree

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- discrimination or hateful conduct
- repeated bad-faith disruption
- deliberately posting private, sensitive, or dangerous information

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
community spaces related to Syncodex.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or restrict participation when behavior harms the
project or its contributors.

## Reporting

If you encounter a serious problem, contact the maintainer privately instead of
escalating it in public.
